    Happy Diwali

    A celebration of light, hope, love, and the victory of good over evil.

    Why Diwali Is Celebrated

    Diwali, the festival of lights, celebrates the victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance.

    The Ramayana Story

    Scroll slowly and follow Ram’s journey home

    Exile

    Ram Goes Into Exile

    Lord Ram began his fourteen-year exile with Sita and Lakshman. Their journey through the forest marked the beginning of a great story.

    Sita’s Abduction

    Sita Is Abducted

    During their exile, Sita was taken away by Ravana, beginning Ram’s search for justice and truth.

    Jatayu’s Sacrifice

    Ram and Jatayu

    Jatayu bravely tried to protect Sita. His courage became an important part of Ram’s journey.

    The Search for Sita

    Hanuman Helps Ram Find Sita

    With devotion, courage, and the help of Hanuman and his allies, Ram continued his search for Sita.

    The Battle

    Ram Faces Ravana

    The battle represented the victory of righteousness over evil. With courage and truth, Ram defeated Ravana.

    Return to Ayodhya

    Ram Returns to Ayodhya

    When Ram returned to Ayodhya, the people welcomed him by lighting rows of diyas. This is remembered as the celebration of Diwali.

    The Festival of Lights

    Diwali reminds us that even a small light can brighten the darkness.

    Each click lights one more diya

    How to Celebrate Diwali

    Light Diyas

    Light diyas to welcome positivity, hope, and prosperity.

    Hover or tap the flames

    Create Rangoli

    Decorate your home with rangoli to welcome guests and celebrate creativity.

    Tap or drag on the pattern

    Share Sweets

    Share sweets, kindness, and happiness with family, friends, and neighbors.
